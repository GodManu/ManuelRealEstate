My Story

A Lifetime Rooted in Los Cabos

I began my career in real estate in 2009, at a time when Los Cabos was still discovering its potential as a world-class destination. What started as a passion for helping families find homes became something far greater — a mission to connect people from around the world with the most breathtaking properties on the Baja peninsula.

Over 15 years, I've built a reputation for honesty, patience, and an obsessive attention to detail. I don't just show properties — I listen to your lifestyle, your goals, and your vision, and then I find the property that fits it perfectly.

Whether it's an oceanfront villa in Palmilla, a marina penthouse in Cabo San Lucas, or a private retreat in the East Cape, I have the network and the knowledge to make it happen.
