About This Property

Perched above the pristine shores of Palmilla, Villa Horizonte is a masterpiece of contemporary design where the Sea of Cortez becomes the defining element of every room. Architect-designed with a seamless indoor-outdoor philosophy, the property unfolds across two levels of hand-finished concrete, warm teak, and floor-to-ceiling glass.

The main level hosts an open-plan living and dining area that flows onto an infinity-edge terrace — a space designed for the golden hour. The gourmet kitchen, finished in Calacatta marble, opens directly to an outdoor dining pavilion shaded by mature palms. Each of the five suites is a private sanctuary, three of which offer unobstructed ocean views from private terraces.
