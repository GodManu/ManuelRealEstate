Frequently Asked

Can foreigners legally buy property in Mexico?

+

Yes. Foreign nationals can purchase property in Mexico, including in the restricted coastal zone (50km from the coast), through a bank trust called a Fideicomiso. This trust is established with a Mexican bank and gives the buyer full rights to use, sell, and inherit the property. I work with trusted notarios and legal advisors to guide you through the entire process.

How long does a typical transaction take in Los Cabos?

+

On average, a real estate transaction in Los Cabos takes between 45 and 90 days from accepted offer to closing. The timeline depends on financing, due diligence, and the complexity of the title structure. I stay actively involved throughout every step to keep things moving efficiently.

What are the closing costs in Mexico?

+

Closing costs in Mexico typically range from 4% to 7% of the purchase price and include acquisition tax, notary fees, appraisal, registration fees, and the Fideicomiso setup. I provide a detailed cost breakdown upfront so there are never surprises.

Do you work with buyers from outside Mexico?

+

Absolutely — the majority of my clients are international buyers from the United States, Canada, and Europe. I'm fully bilingual in Spanish and English, and I have extensive experience guiding foreign nationals through every stage of the Mexican real estate process remotely and in person.
